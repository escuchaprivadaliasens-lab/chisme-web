Together We Launch

No one reaches production alone.

We are a community of independent Android developers who test each other's apps through Google Play closed testing — 14 real days, on real phones — until every one of us reaches production. You test mine, I test yours, and nobody gets left behind.

How it works

Google Play asks new developers for at least 12 testers over 14 days before production. Alone, that is the hardest part. Together, it is just a promise we keep for each other.

1

Join with your Gmail and your app

Sign up below with the Gmail you use on Google Play and the app you are testing. We add you to the rotation so everyone can find and join your test.

2

Test everyone's apps — for real

Install every app in the rotation within 48 hours, keep it for the full 14 days, open it like a real user, and share honest feedback. That is the whole job — and it is everything.

3

Launch — and we celebrate

When your app reaches production, the whole community celebrates with you: your launch certificate goes on your profile and in our hall of achievements. Then you keep testing for the next teammate.

The Commitment

This community only works if every member keeps the same promise. These are our rules — simple, visible, and the same for everyone, founder included.

✓

Use a real Android phone. No emulators, no fake accounts — Google can tell, and it puts everyone's launch at risk.

✓

Install every app in the rotation within 48 hours of its start date.

✓

Keep each app installed for the full 14 days and open it like a real user would — not just install and forget.

✓

Give honest feedback: what worked, what broke, what confused you. A kind truth helps more than silence.

!

If you stop testing, your own app leaves the rotation until you are back. Fair for everyone, no exceptions.
